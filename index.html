Academic profile

Early-career researcher in variational analysis and nonsmooth optimization (Ph.D. 2023, Vietnam National University, Ho Chi Minh City): higher-order generalized differentiation, optimality conditions, and sensitivity analysis for set-valued, parametric, and equilibrium problems. Since the Ph.D.: four journal articles (J. Optim. Theory Appl., Optimization, RAIRO Oper. Res.) and three preprints opening new lines of work with B. S. Mordukhovich, N. M. Nam, and T. H. Cao; principal investigator of two funded projects and co-investigator of a NAFOSTED project (2026–2028). Visiting Researcher at VIASM, Aug.–Nov. 2026.
